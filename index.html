About RoseGlowGuides

RoseGlowGuides is an independent beauty guide that helps readers navigate skincare, makeup, haircare, and fragrance without the noise. We don't stock or ship anything ourselves — every product we point you to is sold and fulfilled by our retail partner, StrawberryNet, a global beauty retailer offering 800+ brands and free worldwide shipping.

Our goal is simple: help you find genuine, well-reviewed products at a fair price, and always be upfront about how we make money doing it.

How We Review

We curate rather than manufacture — our picks are based on StrawberryNet's bestseller rankings, publicly available ingredient information, and brand reputation, not in-house product testing. We update recommendations as bestseller lists and ingredient research evolve.

Where we haven't personally tested a product, we say so. Reader reviews and testimonials on this site that aren't explicitly marked as verified are illustrative examples, not claims about real customer feedback.
